Chapter 05 · From me to you

A little letter.

Dear Srishti,

Pata hai, kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon ki Class 10 ke us tuition wale din se lekar aaj tak kitna kuch change ho gaya. Lekin ek cheez jo hamesha special rahi, woh hai hamari friendship.

Teacher's Day ke aas-paas hui woh pehli mulaqat, class ka last bench, tuition ke baad ka khana-peena, bina baat ke hasi-mazaak aur har saal 5 September ko milne ki tradition — ye sab mere liye sirf memories nahi, balki hamari story ke favourite chapters hain.

Thank you har us moment ke liye jo itna simple tha, phir bhi yaadgaar ban gaya. I hope life tumhe bahut saari happiness, success aur beautiful experiences de. Aise hi khush rehna, apni smile aur apna pagalpan kabhi mat khona.

And yes, abhi toh aur bhi bahut saari memories banana baaki hain. Happy Birthday, Srishti. Here's to our friendship and all the years ahead!

— Kanak
